# ETF sector allocation & concentration study — 89 funds

Answer: concentration is structural. The same handful of mega-caps appears
in roughly one in five funds, and NVIDIA carries the highest average
top-10 weight in the database. Every number is recomputed from the
keyless ETF dataset at build time.

## Most widely held tickers

- CORP — in 28 funds, avg top-10 weight 0.89%
- UST — in 20 funds, avg 16.39%
- AAPL — in 19 funds, avg 7.93%
- MSFT — in 19 funds, avg 7.74%
- NVDA — in 19 funds, avg 8.58% (heaviest single-stock weight)
- AVGO — in 18 funds, avg 3.59%
- LLY — in 16 funds, avg 2.90%

## Category sector tilts

- US Equity (16 funds): Technology 28.9%, Financials 14.2%, Healthcare 12.8%
- Emerging Markets (17 funds): Financials 23.2%, Technology 20.8%, Energy 10.4%
- International Equity (13 funds): Financials 22.3%, Industrials 15.9%, Healthcare 11.4%

US equity funds tilt Technology-first; Emerging Markets and International
funds tilt Financials-first — the sector story inverts outside the US.

## Methodology

Source: the keyless [ETF database download](https://wyattau.com/data/etf-database.json)
(89 funds). Aggregates are computed at build time — every rebuild of the
site re-derives the tables from the current dataset. Averages are
unweighted means across the funds reporting each sector or holding. This
is an allocation-structure analysis, not investment advice.
