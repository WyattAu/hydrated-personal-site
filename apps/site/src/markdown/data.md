# World Data, reference datasets

The machine-readable files powering the dashboards on wyattau.com:
keyless JSON downloads, no authentication.

## What datasets are available?

- [ETF database](https://wyattau.com/data/etf-database.json), 89 funds
  with ticker, name, category, sector allocation, region allocation, and
  top holdings. Powers the ETF Intelligence page.
- [SEO audit (site-wide)](https://wyattau.com/data/seo-audit.json), the
  site crawling itself: pages crawled, issues, warnings, passes, category
  scores, recommendations.
- [SEO per-page metrics](https://wyattau.com/data/seo-pages.json) ,
  per-URL status, title/description lengths, h1 count, word count, fetch
  time, issues.

## Can I use the data?

Yes, keyless JSON, free to download. The ETF database powers the ETF
Intelligence page; attribution appreciated but not required. Related
pages: [ETF Intelligence](https://wyattau.com/etf) |
