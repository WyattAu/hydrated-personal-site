A permissive open-source license derived from Apache-2.0, redesigned for improved readability and enhanced patent protections. Features explicit clause declarations that are accessible to non-lawyers while maintaining legal robustness.

## Key Features

- **Improved Readability** — Written for developers, not just lawyers
- **Explicit Patent Grants** — Clear patent retaliation provisions
- **Apache-2.0 Compatible** — Drop-in replacement for Apache-2.0 licensed projects
- **Legal Review** — Designed with input from IP legal professionals
